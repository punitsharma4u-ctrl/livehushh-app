Data Deletion Request

You have the right to request deletion of your LiveHushh account and all associated personal data. We will process your request within 30 days and confirm via email once complete.

📧 How to Request Deletion

1
Click the button below to open an email to our privacy team at info@livehushh.com
2
Include your registered email address and what you'd like deleted (account, order history, or all data)
3
We'll confirm receipt within 2 business days and complete deletion within 30 days
